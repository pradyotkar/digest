🤖 AI Agent Ecosystem

1. 🚨 "BadHost" Vulnerability Threatens Millions of AI Agents

CVE-2026-48710 · Severity 7/10 (High) · May 22, 2026

A critical host-header injection flaw in Starlette (the ASGI core behind FastAPI) lets unauthenticated attackers bypass path-based authentication middleware. Dubbed BadHost, the vulnerability affects virtually the entire Python AI serving stack: vLLM, LiteLLM, MCP servers, FastAPI agent frameworks, and model management dashboards. Fixed in Starlette 1.0.1 — all agent builders should update immediately.

→ Ars Technica Coverage  |  → Technical Deep Dive

4. 🔬 Study: Why AI Agent Teams Often Fail to Work Together

May 2026 · Science News

New research highlights that multi-agent AI systems frequently break down in real-world settings despite performing well in controlled benchmarks. The study identifies coordination failures, priority conflicts, and "agent hallucination cascades" as key failure modes when agents must collaborate on complex tasks. Findings are particularly relevant as enterprises rush to deploy agent swarms for finance, science, and operations.
