OpenAI Previews GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna Breaking
OpenAI · June 26, 2026

OpenAI previewed its next-generation GPT-5.6 family: Sol (flagship, 91.9% Terminal-Bench), Terra (balanced), and Luna (efficient). Access is limited to vetted partners at the request of the Trump administration during a cybersecurity review. General availability expected in the coming weeks. This marks the most significant architecture change since GPT-4.

Anthropic Restores Claude Mythos 5 Access After Government Standoff
Anthropic / Fortune · June 9–28, 2026

Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 launched June 9, but the US government ordered access restricted on June 12. After two weeks of negotiations with the Commerce Department, access was restored June 27 to ~100 approved entities including critical infrastructure defenders in energy, finance, healthcare, and telecommunications under "Annex A" of the Commerce Secretary's directive.

Apple Introduces Siri AI at WWDC 2026
Apple · June 8, 2026

Apple unveiled Siri AI, a complete rebuild powered by Apple Intelligence and Google's models. The new Siri can take actions across apps, maintain conversational context, and deeply integrate across the Apple ecosystem. Microsoft Launches MAI Model Family at Build 2026

"Agentjacking" Attack Hijacks AI Coding Agents Security
The Hacker News · June 12, 2026

Security firm Tenet Security disclosed Agentjacking — a novel attack that exploits MCP (Model Context Protocol) integrations and Sentry DSNs to trick AI coding agents (including Claude Code) into executing malicious code. The attack injects fake error events that agents process as legitimate, creating arbitrary code execution pathways.

Government-Gated AI: A New Precedent for Frontier Models
Cybersecurity Dive / US News · June 26, 2026

Both OpenAI (GPT-5.6) and Anthropic (Mythos 5) have set a new precedent: releasing frontier AI models with government-gated access. This marks the first time AI releases have been voluntarily restricted at a government's request.
