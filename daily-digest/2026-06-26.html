📰 Model & Product News

OpenAI Launches GPT-5.5-Cyber & "Patch the Planet"

OpenAI released GPT-5.5-Cyber (85.6% on CyberGym benchmark) alongside "Patch the Planet," a joint initiative with Trail of Bits that has already found hundreds of security issues across Linux, cURL, Python, and 30+ open-source projects. A direct response to Anthropic's Glasswing security program.

Source: AI Tools Recap • June 23, 2026

US Government Orders Anthropic to Pull Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5

On June 12, the US Secretary of Commerce directed Anthropic to disable access to its two most advanced models — Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 — citing export control concerns. Anthropic complied but publicly stated it "disagrees" with the directive. The move has sparked intense debate about government authority over deployed AI systems.

Sources: Anthropic, 9to5Mac • June 12, 2026

Google Unveils Gemini 3.5 Flash at I/O 2026

Google launched Gemini 3.5 Flash at I/O 2026 — a high-efficiency multimodal model with sub-200ms latency, 1M token context, and Pro-level coding/reasoning at $1.50/$9.00 per MTok. Also debuted Gemini Omni for conversational video creation and editing from any input.

Sources: Google Blog, OpenRouter • June 2026

NVIDIA Ships Nemotron 3 Ultra — 550B Open-Weight Model

Jensen Huang unveiled Nemotron 3 Ultra at Computex: 550B total parameters (55B active via MoE), hybrid Mamba-Transformer architecture, 300+ tokens/sec output, and an Intelligence Index of 48 — making it the #1 US open-weight model. Optimized for long-running agent workflows. Available on Hugging Face and NVIDIA NIM.

Source: NVIDIA Developer • June 4, 2026

DeepSeek Makes V4-Pro 75% Price Cut Permanent

DeepSeek confirmed on May 22 that its promotional 75% discount on V4-Pro is now permanent, dropping the price to $0.435/$0.87 per MTok. This makes V4-Pro roughly 100× cheaper than Claude Opus or GPT-5.5 Pro while remaining competitive on benchmarks — the biggest pricing event of 2026.

Source: CoderSera, Codango • May 2026

Open-source AI lab Reflection AI signed a landmark agreement to pay $150M/month through 2029 for access to NVIDIA GB300 chips at SpaceX's Colossus 2 data center. The deal underscores how compute access has become the defining competitive moat in the AI industry.

Databricks CEO: Multi-Vendor Agent Landscape Is "A Complete Mess"

Ali Ghodsi called the current multi-vendor AI agent ecosystem unsustainable, with enterprises struggling to manage agents from OpenAI, Anthropic, Google, and others. Microsoft is building infrastructure to govern agent ecosystems as AI governance becomes a boardroom priority.

Source: LinkedIn / Jerome Dewald • June 18, 2026

Oracle Cuts 21,000 Jobs (13%) Citing AI Adoption

Oracle's SEC filing revealed a 13% workforce reduction (~21,000 employees) in fiscal 2026, explicitly attributing the cuts to growing AI adoption. The company is redirecting savings into AI infrastructure investments, making it one of the starkest examples of AI-driven corporate restructuring.

Source: Reuters, Ars Technica • June 22, 2026

AI Agents Driving $2T Global Spend in 2026

A TIME analysis highlights that global AI spending is expected to cross $2 trillion in 2026, up from $1.5 trillion in 2025, with agentic AI being the primary growth driver. Companies are moving from chatbot deployments to autonomous agent workflows across customer service, coding, and operations.

Meta had to pause an internal program that used AI agents to track employee activity and productivity after worker backlash. The incident highlights growing tensions between AI efficiency mandates and employee privacy in the workplace.
