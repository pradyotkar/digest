📰 Model News

GPT-5.6 Leak: OpenAI's Next Model Imminent

Developers have spotted gpt-5.6 in OpenAI Codex rollout logs under the codename iris-alpha. Leaks point to a 1.5M token context window, improved agentic coding, and cleaner frontend UI generation. Reports suggest a release as early as this week (June 25–27, 2026). ChatGPT 5.6 is expected in Mini, Standard, and Pro tiers.

Sources: Geeky Gadgets · AI News Today

Anthropic's Mythos Found Flaws in Classified US Systems "In Hours"

During a red-team exercise, Anthropic's Mythos model penetrated classified US government systems in hours — not weeks. NSA chief Joshua Rudd confirmed to Congress: "It broke into almost all of our classified systems." The US Commerce Department subsequently restricted access to Anthropic's Fable 5 and Mythos 5 models under new export controls.

Sources: Euronews · Malay Mail

Five Eyes Alliance Warns AI Cyberattack Capabilities Are "Months Away"

The Five Eyes intelligence alliance (US, UK, Canada, Australia, NZ) issued a rare joint warning that frontier AI models will "fundamentally transform both offensive and defensive cyber capabilities" within months, not years. The statement cited models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and others as approaching dangerous capability thresholds.

Sources: Cyberscoop · Democracy Now

China's GLM 5.2: Open-Weight Model Rivals GPT-5.5 at ⅙ the Cost

Zhipu AI (Z.ai) released GLM 5.2 on June 13 — an open-weight, MIT-licensed model with a 1M-token context window and coding benchmarks approaching GPT-5.5 and Claude Opus 4.8. Uses a Mixture-of-Experts architecture with "High" and "Max" thinking modes. Already being called a potential "DeepSeek moment" for the open-source ecosystem.

Sources: DataNorth · DEV Community

DeepSeek Makes V4 Pro 75% Price Cut Permanent

DeepSeek confirmed that its promotional 75% discount on V4 Pro is now the permanent rate as of June 1, 2026. The new pricing — $0.435 input / $0.87 output per million tokens — makes V4 Pro roughly one-tenth the cost of GPT-5.5 Pro's output pricing. Combined with V4 Flash at $0.14/$0.28, DeepSeek remains the cheapest frontier-class API available.

Sources: StackFutures · Apidog
