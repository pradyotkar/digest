Google Makes Gemini 4 Argon Globally Available

TOP STORYModelsAgentsPricing

Google says Gemini 4 Argon is now available globally to developers and enterprises through the Gemini API, with access also rolling out to eligible Google AI subscribers. The launch price is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached input discounted 95%; Google describes these as introductory rates. Its announcement positions Argon for complex, multi-step work across coding, finance, law, cybersecurity and science, and cites a one-million-token unified input/output limit. Treat performance and productivity figures in the launch post as company-reported claims.

American Express Proposes an Intent Check Before Agent Purchases

Agent safetyPaymentsApplications

American Express introduced an Intent Intelligence Framework intended to capture a customer’s purchase instructions and constraints—such as merchant, budget and product preferences—before an AI agent checks out. TechCrunch reports that the system creates an intent identifier and proof-of-intent token used to obtain a payment credential, rather than exposing the customer’s card number. If adopted broadly, this approach would make authorization boundaries explicit at the transaction layer instead of relying only on an agent’s prompt or general account access.

Publisher: TechCrunch — “Amex introduces a framework to prevent AI agents from making unauthorized purchases” (published October 13, 2026). Retrieved October 14, 2026.
