📰 Model News

OpenAI Previews GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra & Luna

OpenAI · June 26, 2026

OpenAI previewed its next-generation model family: Sol (flagship), Terra (2× lower cost), and Luna (high-volume). Sol Ultra mode offers extended compute for maximum quality. Access is limited to trusted partners per U.S. government request. Terminal-Bench 2.1 leader at 91.9%.

OpenAI Slashes o3 Pricing by 80%

Multiple sources · June 2026

OpenAI reduced o3 reasoning model pricing from $10 → $2 per MTok input and $40 → $8 per MTok output — an 80% cut. This makes o3 competitive with GPT-4.1 on price while offering superior reasoning capabilities.

Claude Opus 4.7 Ships with 13% Coding Gain

Anthropic · April 16, 2026

Anthropic launched Opus 4.7 with a 13% improvement over Opus 4.6 on its 93-task coding benchmark. Pricing held flat at $5/$25 per MTok. The model introduces a new tokenizer that inflates token counts by up to 35% for some workloads.

Gemini 3.5 Flash Launches at Google I/O 2026

Google · May 19, 2026

Google launched Gemini 3.5 Flash at I/O — its first model combining "frontier intelligence with action." Priced at $1.50/$9.00 per MTok (3× the previous Flash), it powers the new Gemini Spark personal AI agent. Google also introduced Gemini Omni and reduced AI Ultra subscription pricing.

DeepSeek V4 Arrives: 1M Context at Record-Low Prices

DeepSeek · April 24, 2026

DeepSeek launched V4 Flash and V4 Pro with unified 1M token context and 384K max output. V4 Flash at $0.14/$0.28 per MTok is 18× cheaper than GPT-5. The V4 Pro 75% promotional discount ended May 31 — prices now at full $1.74/$3.48.

Google is rolling out information agents to Pro/Ultra subscribers, with Spark (24/7 personal agent) for Ultra subscribers and Halo for Android later this year. Gemini 3.5 Flash powers these new agentic experiences.

Google Cloud's 2026 AI Agent Trends report declares "the era of simple prompts is over." Enterprises are now deploying agents that orchestrate complex, end-to-end workflows semi-autonomously. The defining opportunity of 2026 is speed-to-value through agentic AI.
