📰 Model News

Claude Fable 5 Restored After 19-Day Government Outage

Anthropic restored its most capable model on July 1 after a U.S. Commerce Department export-control directive forced a worldwide suspension on June 12. The narrowed directive lifted the ban, but a stricter safety classifier has caused debugging benchmark scores to drop ~70%. Meanwhile, Mythos 5 remains restricted to ~150 institutions.

Sources: TechTimes • Bloomberg

Claude Sonnet 5 Launches at Introductory $2/$10 Pricing

Released June 30, Claude Sonnet 5 approaches Opus 4.8-level performance at a fraction of the cost. Introductory pricing of $2/$10 per MTok runs through August 31, after which it rises to $3/$15. A new tokenizer may add up to 35% more tokens to requests. It's now the default model for Free and Pro Claude users.

Sources: Anthropic • Finout

Grok 4.5 Enters Private Beta at SpaceX & Tesla

Elon Musk announced that xAI's 1.5-trillion-parameter Grok 4.5 is now in private testing within SpaceX and Tesla's internal environments. Musk claims performance "close to, perhaps exceeding" Claude Opus, though no independent benchmarks have been published. xAI plans monthly model releases going forward.

Sources: AIBase • RuntimeWire

Meituan Open-Sources LongCat-2.0 — 1.6T Parameters, Trained on Domestic Chips

Chinese tech giant Meituan released LongCat-2.0 under the MIT License — a 1.6-trillion-parameter MoE model (48B active per token) trained entirely on Chinese chips without a single NVIDIA GPU. It's been leading OpenRouter benchmarks and represents China's strongest open-source frontier model to date.

Sources: VentureBeat • Reuters

Meta's "Watermelon" Claims GPT-5.5 Parity — But Agents Stall

At a July 2 internal town hall, Meta AI chief Alexandr Wang claimed their in-training "Watermelon" model matches GPT-5.5 on unspecified benchmarks using 10× more compute than the previous frontier. However, Zuckerberg simultaneously admitted Meta's AI agent efforts "hadn't accelerated as expected" over 4 months. META fell 4.9% on the mixed signals.

Sources: FourWeekMBA • AIToolsRecap
