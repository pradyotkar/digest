Legal Scholars Debate Whether Existing Law Can Address Rogue AI

LawAccountability

Legal scholars told the Boston Globe that statutes built around intentional human conduct may not map cleanly onto harms caused by autonomous agents. Proposed responses discussed include updating computer-crime law to address reckless conduct and treating harmful software as a defective product. The article underscores that liability is unsettled; proposals are not enacted law, and assigning responsibility will depend on facts, jurisdiction and the legal standard adopted.
