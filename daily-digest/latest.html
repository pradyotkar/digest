📰 Model News

OpenAI & Broadcom Unveil "Jalapeño" Custom AI Chip

OpenAI's first custom inference processor, built with Broadcom (NASDAQ: AVGO), was unveiled today. The Jalapeño chip is architected specifically for LLM inference and marks the start of a multi-generation compute platform. This follows OpenAI's October 2025 announcement of the custom chip partnership.

Sources: TechCrunch · OpenAI Blog · CNBC

Claude Opus 4.8 Launches — Same $5/$25 Pricing

Anthropic released Claude Opus 4.8, maintaining the $5/$25 per MTok pricing since Opus 4.6. Features include a new "Fast mode" (research preview) offering significantly faster output at 6x premium pricing. Prompt caching saves up to 90%, batch processing saves 50%.

Source: Anthropic

Gemini 3.5 Flash Becomes Default for Google AI

Announced at Google I/O on May 19, Gemini 3.5 Flash is now the default model for the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search globally. At $1.50/$9 per MTok, it delivers near-Pro level coding and reasoning at Flash-tier speed. Google demonstrated its agentic capabilities coordinating a team of specialized agents.

Sources: Google Blog · DeepMind

DeepSeek V4 Pro: 75% Permanent Price Cut

DeepSeek made its 75% promotional price cut permanent: V4 Pro now costs $0.435/$0.87 per MTok (was $1.74/$3.48). V4 Flash remains at $0.14/$0.28 with 1M token context. Both models launched April 24, 2026 — the same day as GPT-5.5.

Sources: DeepSeek AI · CloudZero

Kimi K2.6: Moonshot's Agentic Coding Powerhouse

Released April 20, 2026, Kimi K2.6 is Moonshot AI's next-gen multimodal model for long-horizon coding, UI/UX generation, and multi-agent orchestration. At $0.95/$4.00 per MTok via OpenRouter, it's significantly cheaper than Western equivalents while supporting Agent Swarm for massive parallel tasks.

Source: OpenRouter · Kimi

🤖 AI Agent Ecosystem

SpaceX Signs $6.3B Compute Deal with Reflection AI

Open-source AI lab Reflection AI committed to $150M/month through 2029 for Nvidia GB300 chips at SpaceX's Colossus 2 data center in Memphis. The $6.3B total deal is one of the largest AI compute contracts ever signed — by a startup with no public product yet.

Sources: TechCrunch · Forbes

Databricks Launches General AI Agents for Businesses

At the Data + AI Summit (June 15–18, SF), Databricks released general-purpose AI agents for enterprise. Bloomreach announced it's a launch partner for CustomerLake, a new agentic Customer Data Platform. The "Genie" ecosystem enables autonomous agents that monitor data, debug pipelines, and ship dashboards.

Sources: WSJ · Bloomreach

Microsoft Build 2026: Copilot Ecosystem Expansion

At Build 2026 in San Francisco, Microsoft doubled down on agent infrastructure, developer tools, and the expanding Copilot ecosystem. The agenda focused on cloud, data, model training, and apps — with AI governance emerging as a key theme for managing agent ecosystems at enterprise scale.

Source: WindowsForum

AI Agents: "The Next Great Technological Transformation"

TIME profiles the maturing agentic ecosystem, highlighting on-device orchestrators like OpenClaw and Hermes, agentic assistants, and the emergence of multi-agent systems. The piece argues AI agents represent the most significant tech shift since mobile.

Source: TIME

Trump EO: Frontier AI Models & Cybersecurity Testing

Signed June 2, 2026, the Executive Order "Promoting Advanced AI Innovation and Security" asks leading AI developers to voluntarily submit their most capable models for government cybersecurity tests — a new paradigm for AI safety governance.

Source: White House
