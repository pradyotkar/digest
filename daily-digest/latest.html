Claude can now read your bank accounts: Anthropic ships read-only Personal Finance via Plaid for US paid subscribers

ProductApplications

Benzinga via Yahoo Finance reports Anthropic began rolling out Personal Finance on Thursday to US Claude Pro, Max and Ultra subscribers: users link bank and credit-card accounts through Plaid and Claude reads balances, purchases and upcoming bills across up to 16 US banks and two years of transactions. It explicitly cannot move, manage or invest money—and Releasebot's changelog confirms the beta shipped alongside new Enterprise security and admin controls on November 5.

The guardrails are the story: Anthropic says Personal Finance hub chats are excluded from model training, there will be no ads or sponsored recommendations, and users can disconnect at any time. The long game, per The Decoder, is wiring Claude to licensed financial advisors and regulated firms—OpenAI already ships a similar US feature called Finances. As AIToolsRecap framed the comparison: the chatbots are racing into personal-finance data with read-only mandates, because read-only is what regulators and users will tolerate first.

The data-centre backlash goes continental: 15 countries protested, 200 detained at Microsoft's Amsterdam site

AI PolicySociety

The "Stop Dirty Data Centres" campaign staged two days of action on November 6–7 across 15 countries, demanding moratoriums on new hyperscale builds. The sharpest moment came in Amsterdam, where AP reports protesters broke into a Microsoft data-centre construction site, climbed a crane and set off flares—police detained 150–200 people for trespass. PwC projects $5.6 trillion of cumulative European data-centre capex 2026–2050. Siting opposition is becoming a line item in AI's cost curve.
