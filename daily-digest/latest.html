DOJ's "Super Intelligence Task Force" is up and running—and the Pentagon wants a law inventory first

AI PolicyGovernment

The Register reports the Justice Department's Super Intelligence Task Force (SINTF) is now operational following Trump's October 13 memo. Led by Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton with associate AG Stanley Woodward, it will organize the DoJ's AI-related criminal and civil cases, set enforcement priorities, write prosecution guidance for unlawful AI conduct, and feed legislative proposals designed to prevent "burdensome regulation."

Its first visible move is defensive: Pentagon CTO Emil Michael says the task force will publish an inventory of existing laws governing US AI labs within "a week or two"—explicitly framed as an argument that Congress doesn't need new AI legislation. With 120 days until its risk report is due, SINTF is becoming the administration's answer to a post-midterm Congress that no longer accepts self-regulation.

AI AgentsProduct

NVIDIA's SkillSpector scans agent skills before install—and finds 26% of them are vulnerable

AI AgentsSecurity

The fastest-rising agent security repo this week is NVIDIA's SkillSpector (~25k GitHub stars), a scanner that inspects AI agent "skills" for prompt-injection and data-exfiltration risks before installation, supporting Claude Code, Codex and MCP skill packages. Its headline finding from analyzing 31,132 skills: 26.1% contained vulnerabilities and 5.2% showed likely malicious intent, each scored 0–100 before an agent ever runs them.

It lands as the same ecosystem publishes hard edges: OpenAI's GPT-6 Cyber is gated to verified Daybreak Red customers at $20/$100 per million tokens under a Critical risk rating, Moonshot open-weighted Kimi K3.1 Pro (2.8T parameters, the highest-ranked open model) under a usage-throttled modified MIT license, and Apple added autonomous-AI-agent risk factors to its SEC 10-K. The agent supply chain now has a npm-like malware problem—and the first scanners, price tags and legal disclosures for it are arriving together.
