Google launches Gemini 4 Argon globally, with introductory API pricing

TOP STORYModel ReleasePricing

Google says Gemini 4 Argon is now available globally to developers and enterprises through its API, and to eligible subscribers through Gemini and Antigravity. Launch pricing is $2 per million input tokens and $10 per million output tokens, with cached inputs discounted 95%. Google says those rates rise to $4 and $20, respectively, after November 30.

The near-term discount can make evaluation cheaper, but teams building persistent agents should budget against the higher post-promotion rate and measure full task cost—not just tokens per call.
