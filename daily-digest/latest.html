TechCrunch adds two details worth watching: a ~20% retail allocation, well above IPO norms, and each of the seven co-founder executives — Dario and Daniela Amodei, Tom Brown, Jack Clark, Jared Kaplan, Sam McCandlish, Christopher Olah — selling nearly 3.4M shares, ~$250M each at the $74 midpoint. The listing lands the same week Nvidia hosts its first post-Huang earnings call, and a Yahoo Finance comparison frames the competitive backdrop: OpenAI leans 90%+ on Nvidia at an adjusted operating margin of −109%, while Anthropic hedges across Google TPUs and AWS Trainium at roughly +15%. Public markets get audited numbers for the first time Thursday.
