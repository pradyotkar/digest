Regulation EU Milestone

🇪🇺 EU AI Act Takes Full Effect Today — Fines Up to €35M or 7% of Global Turnover

After a two-year transition, the EU AI Act becomes fully enforceable on August 2, 2026. Article 50 transparency obligations are the headline: all synthetic audio, images, and video must now carry machine-readable watermarks. The European Commission has finalized its Code of Practice on watermarking. High-risk AI obligations have been pushed to 2027–2028 by June 2026 amendments, but today's watermarking requirements apply immediately. This is being called the "Brussels Effect" — global companies must comply to access the EU market.
