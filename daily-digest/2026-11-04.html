Anthropic is developing cryptographic proof of which model produced an output

ResearchTrust & Safety

Anthropic's proposed open-source system, FlashProve, aims to let users verify that a given response came from a specific AI model. The approach uses cryptographic proof and depends on model providers publishing a fingerprint for each release. If workable at scale, it could help verify model identity where outputs are routed through third-party services or could be misrepresented.

Provable provenance does not establish that an answer is accurate; it addresses a narrower question of which model generated it, a useful distinction for audits and high-trust workflows.

Source: TechCrunch — “Anthropic is working on a system to prove AI models’ identity” (published November 3, 2026). Retrieved November 4, 2026.

For education platforms, separating core access from optional AI features is becoming a question of institutional control, not just product configuration.
