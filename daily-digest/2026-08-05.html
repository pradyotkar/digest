OpenAI Slashes GPT‑5.6 Luna Prices by 80% in Escalating API Price War

BREAKING Pricing

OpenAI dropped GPT-5.6 Luna prices by a staggering 80% on July 30 — from $1.00/$6.00 per million input/output tokens to $0.20/$1.20. The mid-tier Terra model also saw a 20% cut, while flagship Sol got a new paid "Fast mode" instead of a price drop. Combined with Luna, Terra, and Sol, GPT-5.6 now spans three tiers optimized for different cost-performance needs.

This move comes as Anthropic runs introductory pricing on its newer models through August 2026, and DeepSeek continues aggressive pricing with its V4 family. The AI API price war is reshaping the economics of building on LLMs — intelligence is getting dramatically cheaper, fast.

Cloudflare Gives AI Agents an Identity and a Wallet

NEW Agents

Cloudflare announced Cloudflare Wallets and cloudflare.pay on August 4, giving AI agents deployed on its network a stable identity and the ability to make purchases online — within limits set by their human creators. This is a foundational infrastructure play for the agent economy.

For the first time, buyers and sellers have the building blocks for autonomous agent-to-agent commerce. Combined with 2026's explosion of agent frameworks and multi-agent systems, this signals that the agent ecosystem is moving from prototypes to real economic infrastructure.

White House Hosts AI Companies for New Model-Testing Framework

Policy
