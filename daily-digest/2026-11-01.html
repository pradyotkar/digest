House committee leaders seek rapid reporting of frontier-model safety incidents

Policy & SafetyU.S. Policy

Democratic leaders of three House committees asked five leading frontier AI companies to report misalignment and safety incidents to Congress within 48 hours of detection, according to the committee press release. They also requested a retrospective accounting by November 11 and faster notice if critical infrastructure is implicated.

The request is not itself a binding reporting rule. It sharpens the policy debate over voluntary disclosure, consistent definitions of an incident, and how quickly governments should learn about failures uncovered in testing.
