📰 Model News

OpenAI Releases GPT-5.6 Under Government-Gated Access

OpenAI began a limited rollout of GPT-5.6 on June 26 after the Trump administration asked the company to restrict early access to government-approved partners only. This marks the first time a U.S. administration has intervened directly in an AI model release. OpenAI executives warned this could set a precedent for a "de facto licensing regime."

Sources: Politico · WIRED · WSJ

U.S. Partially Restores Anthropic's Mythos 5 Access — Fable 5 Still Restricted

After ordering Anthropic to suspend Fable 5 and Mythos 5 on June 12 via export controls, the U.S. government has partially reversed course. Over 100 "trusted" U.S. organizations now have access to Mythos 5. Fable 5 remains fully restricted, but Axios reports limits could lift as soon as next week.

Sources: Reuters · NYT · Axios

Anthropic Accuses Alibaba of "Brazen" Distillation Attack on Claude

Anthropic sent a letter to U.S. officials accusing Alibaba's Qwen AI lab of running a large-scale campaign (April 22 – June 5) to "illicitly" extract Claude's capabilities through thousands of fraudulent accounts, generating over 28.8 million exchanges. The move underscores escalating tensions over AI model IP theft.

Sources: Reuters · CNBC · Bloomberg

Kimi K2.6: Open-Weights Model Hits #1 on SWE-bench Pro

Moonshot AI's Kimi K2.6 — a 1-trillion parameter MoE model with 32B active parameters — scored 58.6% on SWE-bench Pro, surpassing Claude Opus 4.6 and GPT-5.4. Its Agent Swarm primitive can fan out to 300 sub-agents across 4,000 coordinated steps. Weights available on Hugging Face under Modified MIT License.

Sources: OpenRouter · CoderSera

Google Gemini 3.5 Flash Launches — 4× Faster, Rivals Flagships

Released May 19, Gemini 3.5 Flash outperforms Gemini 3.1 Pro on coding and agentic benchmarks while running at roughly 2× the output throughput. Priced at $1.50/$9.00 per MTok. Google also announced Gemini Spark, a persistent personal agent interface built on 3.5 Flash, at I/O 2026.

Sources: Google DeepMind · Zypa

🤖 AI Agent Ecosystem

OpenAI Deprecates Assistants API, Pushes Agents SDK

The Assistants API will be fully retired on August 26, 2026. OpenAI has launched a new Agents SDK and the Responses API as replacements, signaling a strategic pivot from conversational assistants to autonomous agent workflows. Developers are urged to migrate immediately.

Sources: SocialCrawl · BigHat Group

Huawei Transforms HarmonyOS into AI-Native Agent Operating System

At MWC Shanghai 2026, Huawei announced a fundamental shift: HarmonyOS will embed intelligent agents at the system layer rather than as standalone apps. The company framed the transition from 4G consumer internet to 5G-A's AI agent era as the most consequential generational shift in mobile.

Source: Peerlist

OpenAI Establishes Robotics Division for Embodied Intelligence

OpenAI has formally created a robotics division, signaling a major push toward embodied intelligence and real-world AGI deployment. This comes alongside broader industry moves toward physical AI, with Onsemi acquiring Synaptics for $7B to push into the space.

Source: Peerlist

Agentic AI Shifts from Buzzword to Infrastructure Layer

The June 2026 agent ecosystem now includes standardized agent-building APIs, tool-use protocols, computer-use capabilities, multi-agent orchestration systems, and enterprise governance frameworks. The ecosystem is rapidly consolidating around a few key platforms.

Source: Billion Hopes
