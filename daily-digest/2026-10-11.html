Nadella Calls for an AI “Emergency Brake” and Independent Controls

TOP STORYSafetyAgents

Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella argued that organizations should treat advanced models as potential insider risks and build containment around them from the start. His proposed safeguards include an authorized human able to pause or stop a model mid-task, tamper-resistant records of meaningful actions, testing, independent controls and incident disclosure. This is a design position, not a newly announced Microsoft product; the practical point is to keep authority outside the model.

Only 3.6% of Reviewed Chinese Model Releases Had Public Safety-Test Results

PolicySafety

A SemiAnalysis review of 857 model releases from nine major Chinese AI developers found that 31 had public safety-evaluation results that could be matched to a specific model; just nine had results available by launch. The finding measures public disclosure, not whether companies conducted private testing. It underscores the gap between safety work happening internally and evidence available to users, researchers and policymakers.
