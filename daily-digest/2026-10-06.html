Jeppesen Activates 150 AI Agents for Airline Operations

TOP STORYAgentsAviation

Jeppesen has activated an initial 150 AI agents in its ForeFlight Airflow operations platform, supporting early-adopter airlines with crew scheduling and disruption recovery. The system can propose recovery options for displaced aircraft and crews, while keeping the operator in control of how much automation is used.

The safety design is notable in a high-consequence setting: the company says agents rely on certified aviation data, preserve a visible trail, operate within each airline's safety-management system and keep a human in the loop. The rollout is an early test of whether agent coordination can improve operational response without obscuring accountability.

Reflection Previews Beam, a 501B Open-Weight Model Built for Agents

Model ReleaseOpen Weights

Reflection AI introduced Beam, a sparse Mixture-of-Experts model with 501 billion total parameters and 23 billion active per token, tuned for coding, reasoning and agentic work. The company says its early benchmarks put Beam near larger open models while using substantially less inference compute; those comparisons are company-reported, and the model remains in final red-teaming.

Weights, a technical report and developer artifacts are planned for later this month under Apache 2.0. If the release meets its stated goals, it could give developers another path to capable self-hosted agent systems without paying the full inference cost of dense frontier models.

Reka's Rho-1 Tries to Collapse Text, Image, Video and Action into One Model

Research PreviewMultimodal

Reka released a research preview of Rho-1, a 19-billion-parameter model trained to understand and generate text, images and video while also reasoning over them. The company describes a single-model approach in which modalities share one context and state, rather than passing work through separate specialist models and handoffs.

Reka also points toward interactive world simulation and robotic control, where predicted visual futures and actions would come from the same model. These are ambitious company claims, but the direction is clear: developers are probing whether tighter multimodal integration can cut latency and preserve context across perception and action.

OpenAI Plans to Watermark ChatGPT Text in the EU

PolicyTransparency

OpenAI will begin watermarking ChatGPT-generated text in the European Union, according to TechCrunch. The announcement puts provenance and disclosure mechanisms in focus as AI-generated material becomes harder to distinguish from human writing and regulatory expectations for transparency grow.

Watermarking can support detection, but it is not a complete provenance solution: copied, transformed or paraphrased text may not retain a reliable signal. Practical impact will depend on implementation details, robustness and how platforms and institutions interpret the mark.
