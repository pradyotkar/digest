Gemini 3.5 Pro Delayed — Google Holds Back Flagship Over Quality Concerns

Google has kept Gemini 3.5 Pro in Vertex preview entering mid-July. Early enterprise testers flagged excessive token consumption on agentic tasks, underwhelming extended coding performance, and multi-step reasoning that fell short of the Pro-tier bar. Google chose to delay rather than ship a flagship with documented performance issues.

Source: Build Fast with AI · July 6, 2026

Anthropic Launches Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5 — New Premium Tiers at $10/$50 per MTok

Anthropic introduced two new top-tier models: Claude Fable 5 and Claude Mythos 5 (limited availability), both priced at $10 input / $50 output per million tokens — double the Opus tier. Fable 5 usage transitions from included subscription usage to credit-billed on July 8. Claude Opus 4.8 launched May 28 at the same $5/$25 rate as Opus 4.7.

Source: Anthropic Platform Docs · July 2026

Claude Sonnet 5 at Introductory $2/$10 — Price Rises to $3/$15 on September 1

Claude Sonnet 5 is currently available at a promotional $2/$10 per MTok, undercutting Sonnet 4.6 ($3/$15). The introductory rate expires August 31, after which the standard $3/$15 pricing takes effect. Sonnet 5 uses the newer tokenizer (~30% more tokens for same text).

Source: Anthropic Pricing Page · July 14, 2026

DeepSeek Deprecating Legacy Aliases — deepseek-chat & deepseek-reasoner Retiring July 24

DeepSeek announced that the deepseek-chat and deepseek-reasoner model aliases will be deprecated on July 24, 2026. They map to the non-thinking and thinking modes of DeepSeek V4 Flash respectively. Users should migrate to deepseek-v4-flash or deepseek-v4-pro.

Source: DeepSeek API Docs · July 14, 2026

GPT-5.6 Broadly Available — OpenAI Expands Access to Latest Generation
