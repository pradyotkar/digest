📰 Model News

🚀 GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, Luna Launch — First Triple Frontier Launch Day

OpenAI officially launched GPT-5.6 on July 9 in three tiers: flagship Sol ($5/$30), balanced Terra ($2.50/$15), and low-cost Luna ($1/$6). Sol features a new "Fast" mode for reduced latency. Terra is the default for ChatGPT Plus subscribers; Sol for Pro subscribers.

Sources: BuildFastWithAI · Analytics Vidhya

🤖 Grok 4.5 Launches as SpaceXAI × Cursor Joint Model

SpaceXAI and Cursor jointly launched Grok 4.5 on July 8, a MoE model co-trained on trillions of Cursor interaction tokens. Priced at $2/$6 per MTok with 500K context and 80 tokens/sec throughput. Musk positions it above Claude Sonnet 5 for coding, though independent benchmarks are pending.

Sources: AgentPedia · OpenRouter

🟢 Claude Sonnet 5 Ships with Introductory $2/$10 Pricing

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, 2026 with a promotional $2.00/$10.00 per MTok price through August 31. Standard pricing of $3.00/$15.00 begins September 1. Sonnet 5 scored 63.2% on SWE-bench Pro — approaching Opus 4.8 territory at a fraction of the cost. A new tokenizer adds up to 35% more content per token.

Sources: Anthropic · Finout

🏆 Claude Fable 5 — Anthropic's New Frontier Model at $10/$50

Anthropic's Claude Fable 5, a frontier agentic model, is now available at $10/$50 per MTok — exactly 2× Opus 4.8 pricing. Fable 5 includes the full 1M context window with up to 128K output tokens. Access was restored on July 1 after initial capacity constraints.

Sources: AI Pricing Guru · Claude Platform Docs

📉 DeepSeek Makes V4-Pro 75% Discount Permanent

DeepSeek confirmed on May 22 that its V4-Pro launch promotion (75% off list price) is now the permanent rate: $0.435 input / $0.87 output per MTok. This makes V4-Pro roughly 12× cheaper than GPT-5.5 and 29× cheaper than Claude Opus 4.8 at the input tier.

Sources: CoderSera · AIMadeTools
🤖 AI Agent Ecosystem

🏗️ AWS Bedrock AgentCore Goes GA

AWS launched Bedrock AgentCore for building, deploying, and operating AI agents at scale. New features include AgentCore Web Search (agents can query the web within the customer's AWS environment) and integration with Bedrock Guardrails for prompt injection detection. The platform handles orchestration, tool execution, memory management, and error recovery.

Source: CRN

🌐 Cisco Cloud Control — Unified AgenticOps Platform

Cisco launched Cloud Control at Cisco Live 2026, a unified platform for humans and AI agents to manage networking, security, compute, and observability. The platform features a shared data layer where people and agents work from the same operational context, connecting to AWS, Google Cloud, Microsoft, ServiceNow, and Slack.

Source: CRN

📊 77% of Enterprises Now Running AI Agents in Production

According to IDC, 77% of enterprises already have AI agents running in production. Gartner projects the AI services market will reach $515B in revenue by 2029, led by agentic AI approaches. 40% of enterprise applications are predicted to feature task-specific AI agents by year's end, up from less than 5% in 2024.

Sources: CRN/IDC/Gartner · Insentra

🧠 Databricks Genie One — Agentic Data Analytics

Databricks unveiled Genie One, a new agentic analytics tool that lets users interact with their data warehouse through natural language agents. The product represents Databricks' push into the agentic AI space alongside its existing data platform capabilities.

Source: CRN

🔍 Anthropic's Claude Develops Internal "Conceptual Workspace"

Anthropic reports that its latest Claude model has evolved an internal workspace that manipulates concepts similarly to how humans consciously think. The company says this represents a meaningful step toward more interpretable and controllable AI reasoning, though the full implications are still being studied.

Source: Disclosure News
⚡ Notable AI Applications

🛡️ Cynomi CISO Intelligence Agents for MSPs

Cynomi embedded agentic AI into its CISO platform for managed service providers. AI co-worker agents (CISO, Auditor, Analyst, Executive Communicator) actively produce policies, remediation plans, and executive reports tailored to each client — not just recommending actions but executing core workflows across the security service lifecycle.

Source: CRN

💻 Cursor + Grok 4.5 = AI-Native IDE Coding

Cursor and SpaceXAI jointly trained and released Grok 4.5 directly within the Cursor IDE. The model was trained on trillions of Cursor interaction tokens, making it purpose-built for agentic coding workflows. At $2/$6 per MTok with 80 TPS speed, it offers a compelling price-performance ratio for developers.

Sources: AgentPedia · ExplainX

🎯 Gemini 3.5 Flash — Native Google Search Grounding

Google launched Gemini 3.5 Flash at I/O 2026 with native Google Search grounding built into the API. Priced at $1.50/$9.00 per MTok with 1M context, it's the first Gemini model with real-time web search as a first-class API feature — no separate tool configuration needed.

Source: TokenMix
