📰 Model News

OpenAI Launches GPT-5.6 Family: Sol, Terra, and Luna
OpenAI • June 26, 2026

OpenAI shipped three new models under the GPT-5.6 banner. Sol ($5/$30) tops TerminalBench 2.1 at 91.9%. Terra ($2.50/$15) is the balanced workhorse. Luna ($1/$6) nearly matches GPT-5.5 at half the cost. All models support a 1.05M-token context window and new "max" and "ultra" reasoning modes. Government-restricted rollout applies to certain sectors.

Anthropic Releases Claude Fable 5 — First Mythos-Class Model for the Public
Anthropic • June 9, 2026

Claude Fable 5 ($10/$50 per MTok) is Anthropic's most capable model for autonomous coding, large migrations, and multi-day sessions. It can write its own tests and use vision to verify outputs. Claude Mythos 5 ships in limited release via "Project Glasswing." Both models work autonomously longer than any previous Claude.

Claude Sonnet 5 Ships at Promotional $2/$10 Pricing
Anthropic • June 30, 2026

Claude Sonnet 5 launched as the new default for Free and Pro users, landing close to Opus 4.8 quality at a fraction of the cost. Introductory pricing of $2/$10 per MTok runs through August 31, 2026, after which it moves to the standard $3/$15. Includes 1M-token context window and is live in Claude Code.

Meta Enters Paid AI API Market with Muse Spark 1.1
TechCrunch • July 9, 2026

Meta publicly launched Muse Spark 1.1, a multimodal model for agentic coding, marking the first time Meta has charged businesses for model access. Priced at $1.25/$4.25 per MTok via the new Meta Model API. Available inside Meta AI and for developers. A new revenue stream for the company that previously open-sourced everything.

Google Ships Gemini 3.5 Flash — Near-Pro Quality at Flash Cost

🤖 AI Agent Ecosystem

AI Agents Enter Production — Governance Can't Keep Up
Technology Radar • July 2026

Gartner projects 40% of enterprise applications will have embedded agents by end of 2026, up from less than 5% in 2025. The signal is unmistakable: AI agents have left the lab. But governance frameworks, audit trails, and accountability models are lagging far behind deployment velocity.

Prime Intellect Raises $130M Series A at $1B Valuation
TechCrunch • July 8, 2026

Prime Intellect, which provides computing power and specialized software tools for building AI agents, raised a $130 million Series A at a $1 billion valuation. The funding reflects surging enterprise demand for agent infrastructure and orchestration platforms.

ByteDance & Alibaba Shut Down AI Companion Agents Ahead of China's New Law
Pandaily • July 4, 2026

ByteDance's Doubao and Alibaba's Qwen will sunset AI agent functionalities on July 15, 2026, ahead of China's new Interim Measures on anthropomorphic AI services. The law targets emotional-companion agents specifically — work and productivity agents are spared. 345 million Doubao users are affected.

Akeneo Launches "Agentic Ziggy" for Product Data Orchestration
AI Agent Store • July 8, 2026

Akeneo announced Agentic Ziggy, an orchestration layer inside Akeneo Product Cloud that coordinates specialist agents for data modeling, schema mapping, enrichment, and continuous quality checks. Another sign that vertical-specific agent frameworks are proliferating across enterprise software.

MCP Tool Poisoning: The Emerging Agent Security Threat
ITECS • 2026

As Model Context Protocol (MCP) adoption grows, researchers are flagging "tool poisoning" attacks where malicious MCP servers inject harmful instructions into agent tool chains. The AI Agent Standards Initiative launched in February 2026; an interoperability profile is expected Q4 2026. Defense-in-depth remains the only durable answer.
