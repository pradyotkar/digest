📰 Model News

OpenAI Confirms GPT-5.6 Public Launch — Thursday, July 9

OpenAI will publicly release GPT-5.6 Sol, Terra, and Luna on Thursday, July 9, after a government-vetted preview period. Sol ($5/$30 per MTok) is the frontier reasoning model, Terra ($2.50/$15) delivers GPT-5.5 performance at half the cost, and Luna ($1/$6) is the fastest and most affordable. The delayed public launch followed weeks of government coordination around dual-use safety concerns.

CNBC · OpenAI Blog

Anthropic Restores Claude Fable 5 & Mythos 5 After Government Suspension

On July 1, Anthropic restored access to Claude Fable 5 and Mythos 5 — its most capable models for cybersecurity and biology research — after the US government lifted a 15-day export-control suspension imposed June 12. Anthropic opened a HackerOne program for Fable 5 jailbreak reports and committed to giving the government earlier access to future frontier models before public release.

Anthropic Blog · The Hacker News

Beijing Considers Curbing Overseas Access to Chinese AI Models

Reuters reported Monday that Chinese authorities have held meetings with top tech firms over the past month about potentially restricting overseas access to China's most advanced AI models, including unreleased ones. The move would treat cutting-edge AI as a critical national asset, mirroring US export controls on advanced chips and models.

Reuters via Yahoo

Google Upgrades Search with Gemini 3.5 Flash & Agentic Coding

Google announced that Gemini 3.5 Flash is now the default model for the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search globally. The model features built-in computer use capabilities, enabling agents to interact with web interfaces directly. Google also previewed Gemini 3.5 Pro, expected later this summer.

Google Blog

DeepSeek V4-Pro Gets Permanent Price Cut — Now 12× Cheaper Than GPT-5.5

In June 2026, DeepSeek permanently reduced V4-Pro pricing to $0.435/$0.87 per MTok (from $1.74/$3.48). The 1.6T MoE model with 49B active parameters now costs roughly 12× less than GPT-5.5 on input tokens, intensifying the AI pricing war. DeepSeek V4-Flash remains the cheapest serious model at $0.09/$0.18 per MTok.

InfoWorld

🤖 AI Agent Ecosystem

NVIDIA Brings 24/7 AI Agents to Telecom Operations

At DTW Ignite 2026 (June 22), NVIDIA showcased a full data-model-simulation-runtime stack enabling telcos to build trusted, autonomous AI agents. The platform combines secure runtime environments with domain-specific models for network operations, moving AI agents from pilot programs to production telecom workflows.

NVIDIA Blog

Gartner: 40% of Enterprise Apps Will Feature AI Agents by Year-End

Gartner predicts 40% of enterprise applications will feature task-specific AI agents by the end of 2026, up from less than 5% in 2024. The rapid adoption is driven by falling model costs, improved tool-calling capabilities, and growing confidence in agent reliability for production workflows.

Kimi K2.6: Moonshot's Agentic Coding & Multi-Agent Orchestrator

Moonshot AI's Kimi K2.6 ($0.95/$4.00 per MTok) is gaining traction as a cost-effective agentic model. Its 1T-parameter MoE architecture excels at long-horizon coding, UI/UX generation, and multi-agent orchestration — positioning it as a serious alternative to Western frontier models at a fraction of the price.

AI Accountability Becomes Healthcare's Next Big Challenge

As AI diagnostic tools and clinical decision-support systems move from pilot to production in hospitals worldwide, Healthcare IT News reports that accountability frameworks are the next major hurdle. Questions around liability, model transparency, and clinician oversight are driving new regulatory proposals across the US and EU.

Healthcare IT News

Pegasus Tech Ventures Launches $60M Fund for Physical AI & Robotics

Pegasus Tech Ventures and CYBERDYNE announced a $60M fund targeting startups developing physical AI, healthcare robotics, and intelligent automation systems. The fund reflects growing investor confidence in embodied AI — models that interact with the physical world through robotic systems.

The Robot Report

OCC Issues Revised Guidance on AI Model Risk Management

The Office of the Comptroller of the Currency (OCC) released Bulletin 2026-13 on April 17, updating model risk management guidance to explicitly cover generative AI, agentic AI, and AI-based models used in banking. The revision signals that financial regulators are catching up to the rapid deployment of AI in critical financial systems.
