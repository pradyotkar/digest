OpenAI launches GPT-6.1 Terra, targeting cheaper computer-use work

TOP STORYModel ReleasePricing

OpenAI introduced GPT-6.1 Terra on October 26 as a model for visual reasoning, computer use and routine professional tasks. The company’s announcement describes it as fast and efficient; an industry report lists API rates of $1 per million input tokens and $5 per million output tokens, with cached input at $0.05. Those prices are reported as 50% below GPT-5.6 Terra and GPT-6.1 Sol standard rates.

The positioning puts cost-per-completed-workflow, not just benchmark leadership, at the center of the agent model race. Real-world economics will still depend on tool-call fees, task success, retries and human review.

OpenAI’s dots put the always-on personal agent into everyday apps

AI AgentsPrivacy

TechCrunch reports that OpenAI’s dots assistant is available in ChatGPT desktop and mobile apps for Pro-tier subscribers and above, with plans starting at $100 a month. Connected to tools such as email, calendars and a computer, it is designed to work in the background. OpenAI says users can control connected resources and opt out of model training, while the report notes that permission controls are not fully granular.

Always-on access makes permission scope and predictable behavior product requirements, not fine print. OpenAI’s own product description at DevDay framed dots as agents taking on ongoing responsibilities; practical trust will depend on dependable notifications and precise access boundaries.

Sources: TechCrunch — “How OpenAI turned its coding tools into dots, an always-on personal assistant” (published October 26, 2026); OpenAI — “DevDay 2026 Recap” (published September 29, 2026). Retrieved October 27, 2026.
