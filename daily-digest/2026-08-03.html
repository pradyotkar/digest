📅 August 3, 2026 · 🏢 Alibaba Qwen Team

Alibaba's Qwen team has officially released Qwen 3.8-Max, the most capable model in the Qwen family to date. Built on Qwen 3.5's architecture, it scales to 2.4 trillion parameters using a Mixture-of-Experts design. ⚡ DeepSeek V4 Flash Exits Preview — MIT-Licensed, Agentic Gains

📅 July 31, 2026 · 🏢 DeepSeek

DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731 has graduated from preview to official public-beta release. While the architecture remains unchanged — 284B MoE, 13B active parameters, 1M-token context — a round of re-post-training on agent data delivered dramatic improvements.
