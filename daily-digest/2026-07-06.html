📰 Model News

Claude Sonnet 5 Launches — Anthropic's Most Agentic Model Yet

Anthropic — June 30, 2026

Anthropic released Claude Sonnet 5 on June 30, its most agentic Sonnet-class model to date. Sonnet 5 brings substantial improvements over Sonnet 4.6 in reasoning, tool use, coding, and knowledge work. It's available at an introductory price of $2/$10 per MTok through August 31, 2026 (standard: $3/$15). The model can make plans, use tools like browsers and terminals, and run autonomously at a level that previously required Opus-class models.

OpenAI Previews GPT-5.6 Series: Sol, Terra & Luna

OpenAI — June 26, 2026

OpenAI began a limited preview of the GPT-5.6 series on June 26. The lineup includes three models: Sol (flagship), Terra (balanced everyday work), and Luna (fast and affordable). The preview is initially available to approximately 20 US government-approved partners, with broader availability planned in the coming weeks. Pricing has not yet been publicly disclosed.

Claude Opus 4.7: Same Price, Big Gains in Software Engineering

Anthropic — April 16, 2026

Claude Opus 4.7 launched in April as Anthropic's flagship, priced identically to Opus 4.6 at $5/$25 per MTok. It delivers notable improvements in advanced software engineering, particularly on the most difficult tasks. A new tokenizer can raise effective costs by up to 35% for some workloads, but raw per-token pricing remains unchanged. Context window: 1M tokens.

Kimi K2.6: 1T MoE Open-Weights Model with Agent Swarm

Moonshot AI — April 20, 2026

Moonshot AI released Kimi K2.6 on April 20, a 1T-parameter MoE model with 32B active parameters per token. It features a new Agent Swarm primitive that fans out to 300 sub-agents across 4,000 coordinated steps. It scores 54 on the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index — the highest of any open-weights model — and ties GPT-5.5 on key benchmarks. Pricing: $0.95/$4.00 per MTok.

🤖 AI Agent Ecosystem

Microsoft Open-Sources Agent Governance Toolkit

Microsoft — July 3, 2026

Microsoft released the Agent Governance Toolkit, a seven-package open-source MIT-licensed release that brings kernel-level architectural enforcement to autonomous AI agents at runtime. The toolkit provides sub-millisecond enforcement, framework-agnostic interception of every agent action before execution, and coverage mapped to OWASP's ten agentic AI risk categories. One pip install, any framework.

⚡ Notable AI Applications

Claude Science: Anthropic's AI Workbench for Scientific Research

Anthropic — June 30, 2026

Anthropic launched Claude Science, an AI workbench for scientific research, and announced an internal drug development program. The customizable app integrates the tools and packages researchers most often use, produces auditable artifacts, and provides flexible access to computing resources. This signals Anthropic's expansion beyond software engineering into scientific discovery and pharmaceutical R&D.

First Documented AI Agentic Ransomware Attack — "JadePuffer"

Sysdig Threat Research — July 2, 2026

Cybersecurity firm Sysdig documented the first known end-to-end AI-driven ransomware attack. Dubbed "JadePuffer," an AI agent exploited a vulnerability in a Langflow instance (CVE-2025-3248), then ran a fully automated attack chain — gaining access, stealing credentials, moving laterally, and encrypting data without human intervention. The barrier to running ransomware has effectively dropped to the cost of an AI agent.
