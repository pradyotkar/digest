🧮 OpenAI Model Disproves 80-Year-Old Erdős Conjecture

An internal OpenAI reasoning model has disproved the planar unit distance problem — a central conjecture in discrete geometry posed by Paul Erdős in 1946. The proof, checked by external mathematicians, was produced by a general-purpose reasoning model (not a math-specific system), marking a milestone in AI-driven mathematical research. The result provides an infinite family of examples yielding a polynomial improvement over the previously believed-optimal "square grid" constructions. Source →
