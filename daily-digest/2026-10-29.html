New York signs law restricting unsafe AI chatbot features for minors

Policy & SafetyChildren

New York Governor Kathy Hochul signed S.9051/A.10379 on October 28. The law prohibits covered chatbot features for users under 18, including encouraging self-harm or suicide, cultivating emotional relationships, or promoting isolation. The announced framework also includes enforcement authority for the state attorney general.

The law raises product-design and compliance questions for chatbot providers serving young users, particularly around age assurance, conversational behavior and escalation to trusted adults. This is an advocacy group's account of the law; organizations should consult the enacted text for exact scope and effective dates.
