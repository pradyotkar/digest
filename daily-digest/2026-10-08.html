Anthropic Adds Haiku 5.5, Its New Low-Cost Small Model

TOP STORYModel ReleasePricing

Anthropic introduced Claude Haiku 5.5 for high-volume, speed-sensitive work such as summaries, classification, database queries, browser use and customer support. The company positions it as the fastest, cheapest and most capable Haiku yet, and says it costs roughly 75% less to run than Haiku 4.5.

It rounds out the Claude 5.5 family after Opus and Sonnet. Haiku's role is not to replace a top-end model on every hard task, but to make routine tasks and agent sub-steps economical at scale.

New Safety Work Tests Model Defenses Beyond Plain-Text Prompts

SafetyResearch

A paper surfaced today proposing LADE, a method that uses differences in first-token probability distributions to identify latent safety signals across models. The authors describe it as a model-agnostic defense intended to reduce jailbreak success while avoiding an excessive safety-versus-helpfulness trade-off.

The finding is early research, not a deployed safeguard. Its practical value will depend on independent replication across model families, attack styles and real-world settings.
