About

From mainframe Assembler to AI leadership

Started my career writing S/390 Assembler code on Visa's mainframe. Over 24 years, rose to Senior Director, managing the core payment switching system that processes 50,000 transactions per second with 99.9999% availability — the beating heart of Visa's global payment infrastructure.


In 2024, stepped away to found Bayshore Intelligence Solutions, a boutique AI consulting and software development firm. I help startups and enterprises build AI-powered systems — from Agentic AI to intelligent compliance automation. I also advise early-stage startups on product strategy, AI architecture, and go-to-market execution.


Outside of work, you'll find me with a camera in the mountains, exploring new cuisines, or planning the next travel adventure.
