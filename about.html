About

From mainframe Assembler to AI leadership — the common thread is infrastructure-grade reliability.

I started my career writing S/390 Assembler code on Visa's mainframe in 2000. Over 24 years working on Visa's payment infrastructure — including more than 13 years as a Visa employee — I rose to Senior Director, helping lead testing, reliability, and release certification for Visa's core global payment authorization platform.


In 2024, I stepped away to found Bayshore Intelligence Solutions, a boutique AI consulting and software development firm. I help startups and enterprises design and deploy agentic AI, compliance automation, and high-reliability financial platforms. I also advise early-stage startups on product strategy, AI architecture, and go-to-market execution.


Outside of work, you'll find me with a camera in the mountains, exploring new cuisines, or planning the next travel adventure.
