Visa — The Foundation

I started my career in 2000 writing S/390 Assembler code on Visa's mainframe (TPF). That system processed every Visa transaction worldwide — every swipe, tap, and online payment crossed code I worked on. It taught me a level of rigor and reliability that shaped every project since.

Over the next decade at RS Software (Visa contract), I moved from developer to Test Manager, leading QA for Visa's global payments infrastructure. I learned the full stack: ISO 8583 message formats, authorization and clearing flows, settlement cycles, and the operational discipline required to keep a 24x7x365 financial utility running without interruption.

In 2010, I joined Visa Inc. directly as Director, Switching Systems Test Engineering. My team owned end-to-end testing for the core payment authorization engine — the system that decides in milliseconds whether a transaction passes, declines, or routes to a different network. We built risk-based testing frameworks that mapped every code change to specific compliance and regulatory requirements.

By 2016, I was promoted to Senior Director, responsible for the entire switching systems test organization. My team of 100+ engineers across multiple geographies certified every release of the payment core — the same 50K TPS, 99.9999% availability system that processes every Visa authorization worldwide. We led SOX, PCI DSS, and FFIEC audit readiness, maintained business continuity and disaster recovery strategies, and drove AI-powered SDLC automation that cut production defects by over 50% in our domain.

After 13+ years at Visa Inc. and 24 years in payments total, I stepped away in March 2024 to build something of my own.
